Adoption Process

Adopting an animal is an exciting responsibility. Our adoption process helps us make sure that each animal is matched with a safe and caring home.

  1. Review the animals currently available for adoption.
  2. Submit an adoption interest form.
  3. Meet the animal and speak with a rescue team member.
  4. Complete the adoption process and welcome your new pet home.

Foster Program

Foster families provide temporary homes for rescued animals while they wait for permanent homes. Fosters give animals a safe place to recover, become comfortable around people, and prepare for adoption.

Twin Cities Animal Rescue provides guidance and support to foster families throughout the process. Foster care can be a rewarding way to make a difference without making a permanent adoption commitment.

Volunteer Roles

Volunteers help our rescue care for animals and connect with the local community. Some of the roles available include:

  • Helping with feeding, cleaning, and basic animal care.
  • Assisting with adoption events and community events.
  • Helping organize supplies and keep rescue areas clean.
  • Sharing information about animals and rescue programs.
