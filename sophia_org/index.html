Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is dedicated to helping animals find safe, loving homes. We work with adopters, foster families, volunteers, and community partners to give rescued animals the care and support they need.

Our Impact

250+ animals received help from our rescue this year.

180+ animals found new homes through adoption.

75+ volunteers currently support our rescue programs.

How You Can Help

Adopt

Give a rescued animal a permanent home by learning about our adoption process and meeting animals who are looking for families.

Foster

Foster families provide temporary homes for animals while they wait for adoption. Fostering can help an animal become comfortable and ready for its next home.

Volunteer

Volunteers help with animal care, events, cleaning, and other activities that keep our rescue running.

Meet a Rescue Pet

Every rescued animal deserves a chance to find a safe and caring family. Visit our services page to learn more about adoption.
