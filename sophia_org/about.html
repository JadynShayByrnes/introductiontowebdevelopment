Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was created by a group of community members who wanted to help animals in need. What began as a small local effort has grown into a community-supported rescue that works with volunteers, foster families, adopters, and partner organizations.

Our goal is to provide rescued animals with safe care while helping them find permanent, loving homes. We believe that every animal deserves patience, kindness, and a second chance.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We treat every animal and every person we work with respectfully and with care.

Responsibility

We work to provide animals with appropriate care and help families understand the responsibilities of pet ownership.

Community

We believe that helping animals is a community effort. Our volunteers, foster families, adopters, and partners all play an important role.

Community Partners

We are grateful to the local veterinary clinics, animal care organizations, businesses, and community groups that support our work. Their assistance helps us provide better care for rescued animals and reach more people in our community.

We appreciate every organization and community member that contributes time, resources, or services to help animals find safe and loving homes.
